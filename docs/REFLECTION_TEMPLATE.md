# Reflection Template

Use this template for journal-style entries on the site.

## Required

- **date:** `YYYY-MM-DD`
- **theme:** short topic label
- **entry:** 1–5 concise sentences

## Recommended

- Tie reflection to a timeline milestone when relevant
- Keep tone respectful and growth-focused
- Avoid sensitive private details

## Example

**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Theme:** first-day  
**Entry:** I felt excited and a little nervous, but I’m proud of starting this new chapter at MVHS.
