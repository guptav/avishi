# Governance

## Ownership and Roles

- **Repository Owner / Publisher:** final approval authority for published content.
- **Editors:** approved collaborators who draft and update milestone content.
- **Reviewers:** collaborators who verify factual accuracy and privacy compliance before merge.

## Edit and Publish Policy

1. Editor prepares milestone update in Markdown.
2. Reviewer checks facts, links, and privacy compliance.
3. Repository owner (or delegated approver) merges and publishes.

## Accuracy Review Flow

- Validate date, title, category, and status values.
- Confirm references are valid and trustworthy.
- Ensure statements are factual and non-misleading.

## Privacy Boundaries

Do **not** publish:
- sensitive identifiers
- direct personal contact details
- precise private location details
- private records without explicit approval

When uncertain, prefer omission or redaction.
